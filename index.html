About the Author

Writing Across Worlds of Thought

Sarnab Sen is an author whose published works explore different dimensions of knowledge, imagination, literature, and learning. His books span mathematics, mythology-inspired storytelling, classic literature, and children's knowledge.

Through these diverse literary journeys, he brings together curiosity, creativity, intellectual exploration, and the joy of discovery for readers of all ages.
