About the Author

Exploring Dimensions of Knowledge, Imagination & Literature

Sarnab Sen is an author whose published works explore different dimensions of knowledge, imagination, literature, and learning.

His books span mathematics, mythology-inspired storytelling, classic literature, and children's knowledge. Through these different literary journeys, he brings together curiosity, creativity, intellectual exploration, and the joy of discovery.
