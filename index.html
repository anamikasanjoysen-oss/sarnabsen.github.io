About the Author

Sarnab Sen is an independent student author, literary adapter, and mathematics enthusiast dedicated to making challenging academic subjects and classic literature approachable for young readers.

From breaking down advanced problem-solving strategies in the Maths series to translating William Shakespeare's Julius Caesar into clear, modern English dialogue, every title is structured with precision, clarity, and reader-friendly design.
